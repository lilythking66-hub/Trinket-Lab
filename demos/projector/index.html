Trinket Lab · Projector

Lights down, reel on.

Load a stack of photos and they become a roll of film. Let it run past as a filmstrip, or dim the room and project them one slide at a time. Local MP4 videos play in the cinema. Everything stays on your device.

The projector is designed for wide screens. On a phone it is shown scaled down.

Flip the power switch, then press the middle key.
