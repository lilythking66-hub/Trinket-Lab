Trinket Lab

Small digital objects that feel like things.

A growing collection of reusable tactile interactions for the web. Pick one up and play with it.

📸

Instant Photo

Flash, eject, develop, take. Prepared photos and an optional live camera share one photo engine.

 🖨️

Printer

A complete sheet is physically fed through the rollers. Swap the paper content without touching the motion.

 📚

Bookshelf

Take a book out, rearrange the shelf, open it, read, and put it back. Includes optional local text and EPUB import.

 🎰

Gacha

Turn the knob, hear the click, take the capsule and pull it apart. What's inside is always yours to decide.

 💿

Turntable

Import your own music and press it into a record, cover art and all. Place it, switch on, and lower the needle.
